Host live webinars, demonstrate your product, and train your team with [MyOwnConference](https://myownconference.com/). Manage registration, invitations, audience interaction, recordings, and scheduled automated webinars in one account.

Your attendees join in their browsers on desktop or mobile, without installing an app or plugin.

[Create your free account](https://app.myownconference.com/en/signup) · [Explore features](https://myownconference.com/features) · [Choose a plan](https://myownconference.com/pricing)

## Plan, present, and follow up

- Prepare your event with a registration page, email invitations, and automatic reminders. Add your logo and choose the room's colors so your audience recognizes your brand.
- Explain your topic with slides, uploaded videos, or screen sharing. For screen sharing, you install the presenter application on Windows or macOS. It is unavailable on Linux; your attendees do not need it.
- Involve your audience through chat, polls, and tests. Answer questions during your session and use clickable calls to action to guide attendees to your offer or next step.
- Reuse your presentation by recording it as an MP4 file or scheduling an [automated webinar](https://myownconference.com/automated-webinars) from an existing video. Deliver recurring training or product demos without presenting the same material live each time.
- Plan your follow-up using [attendance and engagement reports](https://myownconference.com/webinar-analytics). See who joined, how long they stayed, and which calls to action they clicked. Export your attendee list to CSV or Excel for your team.

You can host as many live webinars as you need, with no platform limit on live session length. Recording duration, storage, and audience capacity depend on your plan.

## Start with a small group or plan a larger event

Try the free Starter plan with up to 5 attendees and 2 simultaneous speakers, without a payment card. Starter is for non-commercial use, includes up to 20 minutes per recording, and adds a watermark to broadcasts and recordings.

For commercial events or larger audiences, choose daily, monthly, or annual access. A one-day plan lets you host an occasional event without a recurring subscription. Enterprise supports audiences of up to 5,000. [Check current prices and plan limits](https://myownconference.com/pricing).

## Connect your workflow and review data handling

On Business and Enterprise, you can use the [public API](https://myownconference.com/support/en/dashboard/public-api) to bring attendee records, attendance data, and chat history into your own systems.

Your events run on infrastructure hosted in the European Union. Review the [Data Processing Agreement](https://myownconference.com/gdpr) for details on how your attendee data is handled.
